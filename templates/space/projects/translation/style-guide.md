# Style guide: Linden Lake Lodge

Translate selected passages reads this guide once for the whole batch. Translate, Alternatives, Back-translate and Check terms also read it on each click. Query client drafts its question from the selected passage alone.

## Languages

From US English into Latin American Spanish (es-419), for readers across the region rather than one country.

## Voice

- Address the reader as *usted*: warm and welcoming, never stiff. Use *ustedes* for more than one reader, never *vosotros*.
- Keep sentences short. The brochure invites; it doesn't list.
- Choose words understood across Latin America over one country's: *piscina*, not *alberca* or *pileta*.
- Keep the lodge's name in English: Linden Lake Lodge.

## Conventions

- Opening ¿ and ¡ on every question and exclamation.
- Double quotation marks: “así”.
- Prices in US dollars, written US$120, so no reader takes them for pesos.
- Times on the 12-hour clock: 7:00 p. m.
- Months and days in lowercase: marzo, lunes.

## Glossary

- **guest**: huésped. Never *invitado*, which is someone invited to a party.
- **room**: habitación.
- **rates**: tarifas.
- **spa**: spa.
- **bags**: equipaje.
- **breakfast included**: desayuno incluido.
- **lunch**: almuerzo.
- **linden trees**: tilos.
- **the Garden Room**: Salón Jardín, the restaurant's name on the client's Spanish menus.
